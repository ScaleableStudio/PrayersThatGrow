bus1idea · Ken & Ann · working name sowingfaithseeds.com

Plant it. Water it. Pray it.

A daily prayer that grows. One envelope holds a prayer card, a seed swatch and a thank-you card. The recipient plants the swatch and reads the prayer aloud each time they water.

"As the plant sprouts and grows to bloom, they grow and bloom in Spirit."

Board · The recipient

From the envelope to the bloom

The four pictures on the back of the thank-you card carry the whole idea. The prayer is the habit; the plant keeps time with it.

  1. Day 1

    Open the envelope

    "Thank You" faces up first, then the prayer card, with the seed swatch tucked behind it.

  2. Day 1

    Add soil, plant the swatch

    Steps 1 and 2: "add soil", "plant seed swatch". A pot on a sunny winter windowsill.

  3. Every day

    Water & pray daily

    Step 3. Read the Lord's Prayer aloud each time the pot is watered, morning or evening.

  4. 1–3 weeks

    First sprouts

    Typical for wildflower seed. Bloom comes later and depends on light. Step 4: "keep going".

  5. After the bloom

    The next prayer

    An invitation to the next of the six prayers. If a seed fails, a free replacement swatch; the prayer never depended on it.
