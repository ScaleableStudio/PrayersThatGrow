How it works

Four steps, on the back of the thank-you card

The pictures travel with the set, so anyone can follow them. You bring a pot and some soil.

  1. Add soil

    Fill a pot with soil. A sunny windowsill suits it best, indoors in winter.

  2. Plant the seed swatch

    Lay the seed paper on the soil and cover it with a thin layer of soil, as the paper's maker advises.

  3. Water and pray daily

    Each time you water the pot, read the prayer card aloud. Keep the soil moist, not soaked.

  4. Keep going

    Sprouts come first, then flowers. Keep praying while it grows.

Good to know

What to expect when you plant

Sprouting takes time

Wildflower seed paper usually sprouts within a few weeks, and flowers come later. Light, warmth and water all play a part, so we can't promise a date.

The flowers vary

The wildflower mix in seed paper changes from batch to batch, so we don't promise one particular flower.

If nothing sprouts

Let us know and we'll send a new seed swatch. The prayer never depended on the seed.

An invitation, not a promise

Each set invites you into a daily habit of prayer. What grows from it is between you and God.
