Board · If this happens

What could go wrong, and what we'll do

From the blueprint's risk list, written as if-then plans: deciding the answer before the problem arrives is what makes a plan hold.

  • Ifthe text engraves too small or faint,

    thenrun the size ladder first; more power or slower speed; a larger size; the fallback font.

  • Ifthree prayer cards don't fit the work area,

    thentwo per run, or a slightly narrower card.

  • Ifthe back of the thank-you card lands off-centre,

    thenflip each card into its own hole, and test before making a batch.

  • Ifengraving harms the seeds,

    thenlow power, and plant engraved swatches with the first batch to check.

  • Iflaser hours run short before Christmas,

    thenstart the samples Nov 5 and launch with the Lord's Prayer alone.

  • Ifthe shop isn't ready by Nov 20,

    thensamples still go out, with a phone number and email for orders.

  • Ifa recipient's seed doesn't sprout,

    thena free replacement swatch. The prayer never depended on the seed.
