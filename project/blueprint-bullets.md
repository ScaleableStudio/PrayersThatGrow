# Blueprint: Prayers That Grow, Christmas Launch

## Objective
Launch Ken & Ann's plantable prayer cards for Christmas 2026. That means 100 free sample sets placed with local
churches and a working store taking orders, built on the settled product, prices and Lord's Prayer set.

## Roadmap

- [x] **Settle the open v3 points** (Ken, 2026-11-04):
  - platform: Shopify;
  - 10+ price: counts the whole order, any mix of prayers;
  - website shows "Matthew 6:9–13" only;
  - step 2: redrawn with the swatch under a thin soil layer;
  - the rest of v3 adopted.
- [ ] **Fold everything into the full blueprint as v4.** Bring v3's vendor file, planting guidance, costing
  lines, gates and risks into `blueprint.md`, and redraw step 2 in the laser files.
- [ ] **Qualify the laser.** Run the five tests in order (size ladder, one prayer card, 3-up, thank-you
  flip, seed swatch). Confirm the work area and how sheets sit on the bed. Record power, speed, passes and time
  per run. Plant three engraved swatches to check they sprout.
- [ ] **Lock supplies and true cost.** Ken picks the production cardstock, #6 3/4 kraft envelopes and
  mailers on amazon.com, with nothing ordered without his say. Fill in the full cost per set: materials,
  postage, labour, spoilage and payment fees. Weigh and measure one finished set and a 10-set order for postage.
- [ ] **Make and place the 100 church samples.** The first 10 sets are the pilot (cuts, flip registration,
  legibility, envelope fit, time per set). Then finish the batch and deliver about 8 sets to each of about 12
  churches with a short note. Log each church, sets given, date, response and orders.
- [ ] **Set up the business as Prayers That Grow** (name chosen 2026-11-04):
  - buy prayersthatgrow.com, and optionally .org;
  - claim the social handles: @prayersthatgrow where free (YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, X); the fallback
    **@4prayersthatgrow** on Instagram and Threads, where @prayersthatgrow is taken;
  - optional USPTO name search;
  - choose the business entity;
  - register for the Texas sales tax permit before the store takes payment.
- [ ] **Build the store.** (Started 2026-11-04: Home, About, Contact with the church request form are built in
  `ScaleableStudio/PrayersThatGrow`; the cart is prepared but not deployed; pricing tests 11/11; nothing hosted
  yet.) Landing, About, Cart/Order, plus a church order form that takes no payment.
  Prices $10 / $8.50 for 10 or more / $7.50 church, plus $1.25 shipping per card. It must pass v3's seven
  acceptance tests before launch:
  - the mixed-cart 10+ price;
  - 9 sets stay at $10 each;
  - a church order is held unpaid, verified by phone, then invoiced at $7.50;
  - $1.25 shipping per card;
  - confirmations go out;
  - orders can be tracked without re-entry.
- [ ] **Finish the content.** Choose the other five prayers and check each fits the card. Settle the final
  thank-you wording (and whether to cite Jeremiah 29:13). Redraw the step 2 picture if approved. Take photos of a
  finished set, a planted pot, a sprout and a bloom. Write honest product copy: seed mix varies; sprouting
  1–3 weeks, not guaranteed; an invitation to prayer, not a promise of results.
- [ ] **Run Christmas orders.** Name who packs and posts and who makes the church phone calls. Keep a small
  stock per prayer. Set the last order date from the USPS 2026 holiday dates. Offer a free replacement swatch if
  seeds don't sprout.
- [ ] **After Christmas.** Review sales, margins and church feedback. Choose the final name and logo, plan a
  seed-paper reorder only if needed (vendor lead time up to 2–3 weeks), and add the remaining prayers.
