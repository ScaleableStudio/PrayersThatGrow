## 1. Purpose and mission

**Known:** a website that sells plantable prayer cards to teach daily prayer. The recipient plants a seed card,
waters it every day, and reads the prayer aloud each time they water. As the plant grows, the habit of prayer
grows: "as the plant sprouts and grows to bloom, they grow and bloom in Spirit."

**Proposed one-line description:** *Plant it. Water it. Pray it. A daily prayer that grows.*

### 4.6 Typeface (Known: Fipty Serif)

- Licensed pack in the font library (`library\Fipty Serif\`, FontBundles Premium License). Letters are turned into
  outlines in the files, so nothing is installed on the laser PC.
- Watch on the test: the capital G's tall right stroke can make "God" read as "Giod" at small sizes; the thin
  strokes may engrave faint. Fixes, in order: more power or slower speed; a slightly larger size; more space after
  G. Fallback face: Gores Sans.
- Licence note: the Premium licence covers the physical product. On the website the font can be used as images or
  outlines, not as a web font file.

## 5. The recipient's experience

**Known (concept):** open the envelope, look over the contents, follow the pictures on the back of the thank-you
card, plant the seed card in a pot of soil, water daily, read the prayer aloud while watering, continue until it
blooms.

**Manufacturer planting guidance (Vendor-confirmed; see §20):** moisten the seed paper; position it just beneath a thin layer of soil; mist daily without overwatering; provide plenty of sunlight; continue caring for the pot. **Proposed illustration clarification:** in the existing four-picture instruction sequence, make the thin covering of soil visibly clear without changing the established four-step layout, cross divisions, or pictures-only treatment. The prayer is read when watering; this purpose remains unchanged.

Proposed, for the About page and the card wording later:
- **Time:** wildflower seed usually sprouts in 1–3 weeks; bloom takes longer and depends on light. State an honest
  range once the seed is confirmed.
- **Christmas timing:** gifts are planted in winter, indoors; a sunny window helps.
- **If a seed doesn't sprout:** the prayer doesn't depend on the seed. Offer a free replacement swatch.
- **After it blooms:** invite them to the next prayer in the set of 6.

## 6. Production

### 6.1 Laser

- **Known:** Ken's laser has completed burns; this product still needs its tests.
- **Work area:** 200 x 150 mm, from the earlier T1 notes. **Open: confirm on the machine.**
- **Open: how stock sits on the bed.** A letter sheet (215.9 x 279.4 mm) is bigger than the work area. If the
  machine is open underneath or has room around the work area, whole sheets can lie on the bed and only the middle
  is used. If not, sheets are trimmed first. Note: a half-letter (215.9 x 139.7 mm) fits only 2 prayer cards
  across, not 3; it does fit 2 thank-you cards.

### 6.2 Layouts per run (built for the test)

| Run | Layout | Footprint | Passes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prayer cards | 3 cards, the 6 in side along the 200 mm axis, 2.5 mm gaps | 152.4 x 147.9 mm (tight on the 150 mm side) | Engrave + cut |
| Thank-you cards | 2 cards side by side, 5 mm gap | 170.1 x 127 mm | Front engrave + cut; flip; back engrave |
| Seed swatches | 2 columns x 3 rows, 4 mm gaps | 156.4 x 84.2 mm | Engrave (+ cut, Open) |

**Thank-you flip method (Known, with the Proposed alignment):** after the front is engraved and cut, leave the
sheet in place, flip each card left to right like a book page, and drop it back into its own hole. The hole holds
it square for the back engrave; nothing on the bed moves between the two files.

### 6.4 Laser test (files ready: `designs/laser-test/`, see its README)

| # | Test | Pass when | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Size ladder (1.4–4.0 mm) | Smallest clear size found; G and thin strokes read well | |
| 2 | One prayer card | Crisp engrave, clean cut through, no scorch; time noted | |
| 3 | Prayer cards 3-up | Fits the work area; cuts don't meet | |
| 4 | Thank-you 2-up front, flip, back | Back sits square on the card | |
| 5 | Seed swatch on seed paper, low power | Clear text; paper not burnt through | |

Record power, speed, passes and time for each in this table.

**Vendor technical qualification (Vendor-confirmed + Recommendation):** Porridge Papers discusses inkjet/laser **printing**, but does not certify compatibility with a laser **engraver**. Its FAQ cautions that processing can affect germination. Before routine production, verify the exact material’s laser safety and evaluate post-engraving germination using the actual test swatches. This supplements—not replaces—Ken’s planned low-power laser test. Do not assume the printer-related FAQ confirms engraving safety.

### 6.5 Files and the generator

- `designs/cards.json` holds every text, size and setting. `scripts/build-cards.ps1` turns it into the laser files.
  Changing a prayer or the thank-you words means editing the JSON and running the script again.
- Each output comes as an SVG (black = engrave, red hairline = cut) and an engrave-only PNG at 10 px/mm.

### 6.6 Making a batch (Proposed workflow)

1. Prayer card runs (all needed prayers), then thank-you runs (front, flip, back), then swatch runs.
2. Check each sheet as it comes off: engrave clear, cut through, no scorch. Reject and redo any bad card.
3. Stack by prayer.
4. Pack (section 7).
